## What the service provides

Authorised users may upload trophy photographs and historical winner records, request AI-assisted readings and illustrations, review names, match entries with an optional club directory, export records and publish a selected honours board. AI output can contain mistakes. The customer checks it before relying on or publishing it. An illustration is a representation, not documentary proof of an inscription. Features dependent on a third-party platform are supplied only when specifically available and included in an accepted order.

Customers keep their rights in uploaded material and their archive records. They give the operator permission to store and process the material, use contracted service providers and display only the version they instruct the service to publish. The operator does not obtain permission to use private archive material in advertising. [Confirm the applicable AI-provider output terms; do not promise exclusive intellectual-property rights that cannot be granted.]

## Accounts and publication

The customer appoints an owner and chooses any editors. The owner manages access, purchases and publication. Each user protects their own credentials and promptly reports suspected unauthorised access. The customer must have appropriate rights and a lawful basis for uploading and publishing personal information, provide relevant privacy information and consider junior winners and requests for correction or removal.

Archives begin private. A public honours board contains an explicitly reviewed version. Later archive edits require a new publication. A public embed remains publicly accessible even if placed inside a password-protected club website. Withdrawal prevents future service access but cannot recall copies already made by visitors. Members-only integration is a separate service, requiring a supported access mechanism; website identity found by JavaScript is not treated as authentication.

## Trophy credits and payment

Core trophy credits are one-off purchases. The first proof credit is separate from paid packs. The current proposed price schedule is 1 credit £7.50; 10 £60; 50 £225; 250 £875. [Confirm final tax treatment and ensure advertised totals, checkout, receipts and VAT invoices agree before enabling sales.]

A credit is reserved when a trophy’s AI work is queued and used once after its first successful AI job. Reading and illustration for that same trophy share that credit. Manual editing, export and access to existing records do not spend additional credits. A known failure releases an unused reservation. An interrupted provider request needs review before another attempt; the attempted AI call still counts towards its allowance.

The free proof currently allows 12 saved photographs, 3 readings and 2 illustration attempts per trophy. Paid archives allow 40 photographs, 12 readings and 3 illustration attempts per trophy. [Approve these limits and the procedure for exceptional work.] Existing trusted legacy archives retain their unlimited allowance. Unused credits do not expire during normal operation of the service. This does not promise that the business operates indefinitely; service closure is addressed below.

Pack upgrades add the difference in credits and charge the difference in pack price. Upgrading a paid 10-credit pack to 50 adds 40 credits for £165; credits already used remain used. A pack cannot be upgraded twice along competing paths. Stripe provides hosted checkout; the archive never accepts card numbers. Credits are added only after a verified payment confirmation, not simply after returning from checkout.
