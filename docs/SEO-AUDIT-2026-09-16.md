## Article update safeguards

The two new revisions apply only to the exact known source revision timestamps. Original posts are backed up on the server, original URLs/publication dates remain, and newer CMS edits take precedence. Updated timestamps advance so the store accepts the corrections and discovery documents can reflect them. Repeated startup is idempotent.
