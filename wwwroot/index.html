Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

Will it read every engraving perfectly?

No. Reflections, damage and unusual lettering can remain ambiguous. The service compares several views, shows confidence and requires human review.

Can names come from honours boards, notes or other records?

Yes. Upload photos of trophy inscriptions, honours boards, championship boards, plaques, printed results, yearbooks, meeting minutes, or handwritten and typed historical notes. They are read together and each proposed winner remains linked to its source photo.

Does it work outside golf?

Yes. The workflow suits rugby, cricket, tennis and bowls clubs, US baseball, softball, football and hockey organisations, schools, universities, museums and other historic collections.

Can we add a trophy that is not already listed?

Yes. New accounts begin empty. Add a trophy, upload its reference photographs and its catalogue illustration is generated in the background.

What happens to member dates of birth and joining dates?

Readable full dates are not retained. Birth dates become a private one-way comparison code plus birth year, joining dates are reduced to year, and the original spreadsheet is discarded after import.

Can we add missing winners ourselves?

Yes. Set an expected date range to reveal gaps, then add or correct winners manually from other club records.

Can we export the finished archive?

Yes. Export trophy details, years, winners, review states and optional member-match information as CSV.
