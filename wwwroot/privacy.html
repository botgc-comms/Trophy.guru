Privacy and cookies

Your archive stays out of our analytics.

Google Analytics is optional on Trophy.guru. It is loaded only after you accept analytics, and rejecting it does not affect the service.

What analytics measures

If you accept, we record broad page categories and successful product milestones so we can understand where the service is useful or confusing. Those milestones include account creation, sign-in, trophy creation, winner-record image uploads, winner confirmation and publication of an honours board.

Google Analytics may also receive standard technical information such as browser and device type, approximate country or region and the referring website's domain.

What is deliberately excluded

The analytics code does not send:

  • names, email addresses or account details;
  • club or member records, dates of birth or membership numbers;
  • trophy names, winner names, notes or search terms;
  • uploaded images, filenames or image contents; or
  • internal trophy, member, winner or club identifiers.

Archive routes are reduced to anonymous categories such as “review a trophy” or “honours by year” before a page view is sent.

Your choice

Your analytics preference is stored in this browser. Use the Privacy & cookie settings button at the bottom of any page to accept, reject or change your choice. If you withdraw consent, Trophy.guru stops sending analytics events and removes the Google Analytics cookies it can access.

Google Analytics

Google provides the analytics service and may process analytics data on our behalf. Advertising storage, advertising user data, ad personalisation and Google Signals are disabled in the Trophy.guru implementation. Read Google's privacy policy for more information about Google's processing.
